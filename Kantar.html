Kantar 10

A weighing program for the scale house

Kantar records every weighing of a vehicle: the car, the driver, the company, the product and the silo are chosen from lists, the weight comes from the scale, and the slip with gross, tare and net weight is printed. Everything is kept in a database that can be archived and reported.

What it does

From the menu

Work

The weighing itself, archiving to the database, the shift of the user, reports and the invoice.

Lists

Cars, a selection list, licences, products, silos, companies and drivers are kept as lists and picked from the form.

Settings

Screens and fields, the database, switching off clients, the menu, users and their access, the station and its communication, and the message sent when something changes.

The slip

A printed slip with the number, the car, the driver, the company, the product, the silo, gross, tare and net weight with their dates, and a barcode.
